Masons Journey — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 5 October 2026 (degree chat rooms, direct messages, meeting attachments, lodge addresses and degree approvals added)

Masons Journey is a private app for members of a Masonic lodge: it holds a map of the Masonic orders, the lodge calendar, and a Learning Hub for practising ritual. This policy explains what the app stores, where, and how to have it removed.

Who is responsible

Masons Journey is published by Lee Roach, who runs it for the lodge and is responsible for the data it holds. Accounts are created by the lodge admin; there is no public sign-up.

What the app stores

  • Your account: your email address, your name, your Craft degree, and whether you are a member or an admin.
  • Degree approvals: when you record a passing or raising, the degree and date you enter go to the lodge admins to approve. Until they approve it your Craft degree stays as it was. The app keeps the date each degree was approved, so it knows which degree chat rooms you can read and from when.
  • Ritual access: which ceremonies the lodge admin has unlocked for you.
  • Your practice: passages you add yourself, the officer part you are learning, and your practice results (scores and missed words), so the app can tell you what to revise.
  • Your progress in the orders: which orders you are interested in or belong to, your lodges or chapters and their addresses, office held, ceremony dates and notes you enter. Addresses are only used to open directions in Apple Maps or Waze, or to share them to another app such as Tesla, when you tap a button; the app never tracks where you are.
  • The calendar: lodge events posted by the admin, and events you add yourself — visible only to you and any brothers you invite. When you invite brothers, they see the event and who invited them, and you see their replies.
  • Meeting attachments: PDFs and photos attached to an event (summonses, menus, seating plans). Anyone who can see the event can open its files; they are stored privately, never on a public link, and deleted with the event.
  • The lodge list: so brothers can invite one another, every brother can see the other brothers' names and Craft degrees (never their email addresses), and the invitation groups the admin sets up.
  • Meeting replies: whether you're attending, dining or sending apologies for a lodge event. The lodge admin sees who replied what; other brothers see only the totals.
  • Lodge chat: messages you post, with your name and the time. Messages in All brethren are readable by every brother in the app. Also any reports you make about a message (seen only by the admin) and the brothers you've blocked (seen only by you).
  • Degree rooms: readable only by brothers at that degree or above, and only messages posted after their degree was approved. The lodge admins can read them to moderate.
  • Direct messages: readable by you and the brother you're writing to. Lodge admins cannot read them. The lodge owner (Lee Roach) can review a conversation if there is a complaint or a safety concern; each review is recorded with the date and the two brothers involved.
  • Notifications: a code from Apple that lets the app send notifications to your phone, and which notifications you've switched on.

The chat, meeting replies and notifications exist to run the lodge's own business; nothing is shared outside the lodge.

The app does not collect your location, contacts, photos (other than ones you choose to attach), advertising identifiers or browsing activity. There are no adverts, no analytics and no tracking of any kind, and nothing is sold or shared with advertisers.

Where it is kept

Your data is stored with Supabase, our database provider, on servers in the European Union (Ireland). Database rules make sure you can only read what you are allowed to: your own practice and progress are private to you, and your own events are seen only by the brothers you invite — even the lodge admin cannot read them otherwise.

An encrypted copy of your ritual and calendar is kept on your phone so you can rehearse without signal. It is sealed with a key held in your phone's secure Keychain, cannot be read while the phone is locked, is not included in backups, and is erased when you sign out or delete your account.

Microphone, speech and camera

  • Recite aloud uses the microphone. Your voice is turned into text by Apple's speech recognition on your device, compared with the ritual, and then discarded. Recordings are never stored or uploaded.
  • Scan a page uses the camera to read text from your ritual book on your device. The photo itself is not kept.
  • Face ID is handled entirely by your phone. The app only learns "unlocked" or "not unlocked".
  • Attach a photo opens Apple's photo picker, so the app only receives the photo you choose, and only when you're attaching it to a meeting.
  • Notifications about new lodge events, chat messages and degree approvals are delivered through Apple's Push Notification service. They carry the event title and date or the start of the chat message. You can switch each kind off in the app, or all of them in your phone's Settings.
  • Reminders are scheduled on your phone. Add to iPhone Calendar opens Apple's own screen. If you switch on Send events to my calendar, the app copies your invitations and events (and lodge meetings if you choose) into the one calendar you pick on your phone, and keeps them up to date. It only ever reads back or changes entries it created; your other calendar entries are never read. Anything that calendar syncs to (for example iCloud, Google, or a Skylight display) is then handled under that service's own privacy terms.

Ask Claude (when switched on)

If the lodge switches on the optional "Ask Claude" assistant, the question you type, and the passage you are working on if you choose to include it, is sent to Anthropic's Claude service to produce an answer. Anthropic processes it under its commercial terms and does not use it to train its models. Nothing is sent to Anthropic unless you use the Ask Claude feature.

How long it is kept, and deleting it

Your data is kept for as long as you have an account. You can delete your account at any time from inside the app (Account menu → Delete my account). That permanently removes your login, profile, practice history, your own passages, your order progress, your personal calendar events and their attachments, your meeting replies, your chat messages (including direct messages), your degree requests and your notification settings. Lodge events posted by an admin stay on the lodge calendar. The lodge admin can also remove your account.

Your rights

Under UK and EU data protection law you can ask for a copy of your data, ask for it to be corrected, or ask for it to be deleted. Most of this you can do in the app yourself; for anything else, contact us as below.

Children

Masons Journey is for adult members of a Masonic lodge and is not intended for anyone under 18.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will appear in the app and on this page with a new date.

Contact

Questions or requests: contact your lodge admin, or use the support link on the Masons Journey App Store page.
